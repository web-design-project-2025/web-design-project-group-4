## Web design project group 04 - Snackademic

Welcome to snackademic! Snackademic is an intuitive, budget-friendly meal planning application designed to help students save time, money, and effort while maintaining a nutritious diet. With features like bulk cooking, easy-to-follow recipes, and customizable meal plans, Snackademic simplifies healthy eating for busy students.

## 🌟 Features

### 🧠 Smart Meal Planner Add recipes to specific dates via an interactive calendar

Add any recipe to a specific weekday.
View all planned meals for the week on calendar.html

### 🥗 Student-Friendly Recipes – Quick, affordable, and nutritious.

Recipes are stored in a structured recipes.json file.

### 🥘 Full Meal-plans made by our best chefs

Grab a meal plan from down below and let us choose for you! With options for all budgets and appetites, we guarantee you’ll find a new favourite meal to put in your lunch box.

### 🩷 Add to favorites

Your favorite recipes are displayed on your account page

### 👤 Log in with google, simple and easy

Google Cloud API ☁️
